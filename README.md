# Fortune’s Weave Recruitment Planner

A standalone, trilingual Part I recruitment planner with a 62-character reference roster. Optimize the 50 Part I companions across four routes. Compare earlier recruitment, lower support requirements and balanced teams, with embedded portraits and route-specific recruitment notes.

- Chinese: `index.html`
- Japanese: `ja.html`
- English: `en.html`

Every page includes all three languages and works offline. No installation, tracking or external assets are required.

## Calculation scope

Renown is a threshold, not a currency. The arrival score sums recruitment thresholds as an approximation of joining time; free recruits count as renown 2. Story joins count toward coverage but not recruitment headcounts or the arrival score. Play each route once. Choose once (50 joins) or twice (43 characters on two distinct routes, seven single-route characters once: 93 joins). The twice goal needs 75 active/free recruitments; its most even distribution is 18/19/19/19. The 12 characters joining outside Part I are listed for reference but excluded from optimization. Support scores sum the listed levels. Gold, items, quests and appearance dates are documented but are not included in optimization.

The screenshot-based thresholds are retained where supplementary guides disagree; those differences are identified in character notes. This is an unofficial fan tool, not an official game guide. Character art and game names belong to their respective rights holders.
